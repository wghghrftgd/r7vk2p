Privacy Policy

This application is a private tool used only by its owner to upload his own video clips as drafts to his own TikTok account(s). It is not offered to the public.

Data received from TikTok: open ID, display name, avatar URL and access/refresh tokens, solely to upload videos as drafts on the owner's behalf (scopes user.info.basic, video.upload).

Storage: this data is stored only on the owner's private server. It is not shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party, and not used for advertising or analytics.

Deletion: revoking access in the TikTok settings removes the application's access; stored tokens are deleted on request.

Contact: open an issue in this repository.
